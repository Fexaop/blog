Building in public fails when every post has to be a launch. Treat it like a **lab log**.

## Minimum viable update

- What changed
- Why it changed
- What broke
- What's next (one sentence)

That's enough for a commit message *and* a blog stub.

## Cadence over perfection

Ship weekly notes if daily is too much. Miss a week? Skip guilt, resume next week. Public work compounds only if it continues.

## Separate research from product

Not every experiment needs a product page. Some posts are pure notes. Label them. Readers will respect the honesty; you'll respect your own archive later.

## Keep the static habit

If publishing requires a CMS login, a CI mystery, or a redesign — you won't publish. Markdown + static export is friction you can live with forever.
